Emberkeep — a top-down arena wave-defence game where you are the keeper of the last lantern. Its flame is your health, your ammo and your light: firing embers costs flame, a foe's touch costs more, and kills feed it back. Hold the light through 15 waves; the Gloomlord comes every 5th. Between waves, choose one of three blessings.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. Move with WASD or arrow keys, aim with the mouse, hold click to fire. Press P to pause, 1/2/3 (or click a card) to pick an upgrade between waves. Best score is kept in localStorage.
